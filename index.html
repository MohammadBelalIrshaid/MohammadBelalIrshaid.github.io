About

If you're building a rehab exoskeleton, moving the leg isn't the hard part. Knowing where the patient wants it to go, a fraction of a second before they move, is. That's the problem my PhD works on.

I'm a researcher and teaching assistant at Khalifa University in Abu Dhabi. My work sits between medical AI and robotics: recognising what a person is doing from wearable sensors, predicting what their joints will do next, and doing it fast enough to sit inside a real-time control loop.

My papers appear under Mohammad Belal and Mohammad Irshaid. Below, each paper is broken into a few steps with an interactive figure, so you can get the idea in a minute instead of reading twelve pages.
